# Prior Work {#sec:prior-work}

> **Status: stub.** Deliberately light for now; to be researched and expanded before assembly.

Areas to cover, with candidate anchors:

- **Systematic option-selling performance.** The CBOE put-write index (PUT) and buy-write index (BXM) and the academic literature analyzing them; the volatility risk premium (implied volatility exceeding subsequently realized volatility) as the documented source of edge in short-option strategies.
- **Covered-call and put-write strategy studies.** Whaley's work on BXM/PUT; Israelov & Nielsen on covered calls and their decomposition of returns into equity exposure and short volatility.
- **Option pricing foundations.** Black & Scholes (1973), Merton (1973) — needed only for the assignment/recovery probability formulas; Hull's *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* as the general-audience pointer.
- **Queueing and inventory theory.** The M/M/∞ (infinite-server) queue and its Poisson steady state — standard references: Kleinrock, *Queueing Systems*; or Ross, *Introduction to Probability Models*. Our inventory process is a discrete-time analogue.
- **What appears to be new here.** We are not aware of prior work treating the wheel specifically as an inventory/queueing system with layered, depth-dependent exit rates and deriving stability conditions for it. This claim needs an actual literature search before publication.
