# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

> **Status: placeholder.** To be written last, once the technical sections are stable — deliberately left empty rather than stale, since the results it must summarize are still being written.

We model the "wheel" options strategy — repeatedly selling cash-secured puts on fundamentally sound assets, taking assignment when it comes, and selling covered calls until the stock is called away — as a stochastic inventory system whose state variable is the depth of each held lot below its own frozen call strike.
